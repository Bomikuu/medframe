Calendar anchoring clamps short months and returns to the selected original day; cent remainders go into the final installment.

Native invoices/payments/refunds/credits remain the sole monetary ledger. Current computed schedule coverage applies credits from the end and net received cash from the earliest obligation. Partial/early payments, refunds reopening coverage and refundable balances use existing native financial entry owners. No generated date or plan action fabricates a payment or moves money. The shared due projection feeds staff statements/payment suggestions, finance aging/dashboard, patient summaries, verified portal statements and kiosk own-bill summaries. Future principal is distinct from due through today and overdue; comparison graphs still show actual native issued/payment/refund events.

Catalog administration uses organization.manage. New service-only read/work grants are additive on existing clinic-administrator/receptionist/doctor/dentist roles; custom roles and existing clinical/financial/device grants remain unchanged. Workflow-only readers receive no pricing/payment DTO fields; patient finances still require existing billing permissions. Staff realm/MFA, live organization/branch/patient authorization, exact versions, device denial, row locks and idempotent replay remain native. Public websites, patient kiosks and delegated doctor-code sessions gain no service authoring or agreement authority. Kiosks display own bills only. No clinical data is automatically copied into website service copy.

Manual plan reminders bind an explicitly selected unpaid obligation and reviewed amount/date/version. Dispatch retains existing consent/source/creator checks and cancels stale snapshots after payment/credit or source changes. No automatic installment reminder scheduler/channel enablement is added; SMS remains preview-only. No AI integration, live messages, payment provider or interest/penalty rules.
