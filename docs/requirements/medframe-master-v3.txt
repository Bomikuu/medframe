# ============================================================
# 0. PROJECT OVERVIEW
# ============================================================

Build Medframe, a secure, modern, multi-tenant healthcare
management platform designed primarily for medical professionals
and their patients.

Project Name: Medframe
Repository: medframe
Tagline: The Foundation of Modern Care.

Medframe is a clinical operating system that combines:

- Doctor and dentist workspaces
- Patient registration and management
- Electronic medical records (EMR)
- Clinical consultation management
- Diagnosis and procedure documentation
- Interactive 3D dental charting
- Interactive anatomical visualization
- Medical annotations
- Treatment planning
- Longitudinal patient history
- Secure medical document storage
- Patient portals
- Appointment scheduling
- Real-time queue management
- Billing and payments
- Diagnostic center collaboration
- Clinic administration
- Personalized clinic websites

INITIAL FOCUS:

1. Dental Clinics
2. General Medical Practices

The architecture should support future specialties without
requiring major changes to the application.

IMPORTANT:

Medframe is a clinical platform first.

Prioritize actual medical workflows and medical records
before implementing appointment scheduling, public websites,
or a website builder.

The first implementation must focus on:

1. Doctors and dentists
2. Patients
3. Medical records
4. Consultations
5. Diagnosis documentation
6. Interactive 3D dental models
7. Treatment plans
8. Clinical history

Appointment booking and queue management come AFTER the
core clinical workflows are functional.

Public clinic landing pages and website customization
come MUCH LATER.

Do not prioritize marketing pages, clinic directories,
or website-builder functionality in the initial implementation.

Build working functionality with real persistence.

Avoid static demonstrations that look functional
but do not actually save or retrieve data.

What the first Medframe release should look like
The first release should establish three connected experiences.
1. Doctor Dashboard
A doctor signs in and sees their clinical workspace.
The main focus is finding patients, starting consultations, continuing draft records, and reviewing pending treatments.
2. Patient Medical Record
Selecting a patient opens their medical record.
The record contains their history, allergies, medications, previous consultations, documents, treatments, and dental chart.
3. Interactive Dental Consultation
The doctor starts a consultation and interacts with the patient's dental model.
For example:
Patient: Juan Dela Cruz
Consultation: Dental Examination
The dentist clicks Tooth 16.
The selected tooth opens an information panel:
Field	Value
Tooth	16 — Upper Right First Molar
Condition	Dental Caries
Affected Surface	Occlusal
Severity	Moderate
Proposed Treatment	Dental Filling
Clinical Status	Active


The dentist records the condition. The model reflects the finding, and the information is saved to the patient's clinical record.
The dentist can then add a proposed procedure, attach an X-ray, and finalize the encounter.
When the patient's record is opened again, the findings and historical documentation are still available.
That is the first complete Medframe experience I would build.
Once these clinical workflows are stable, we'll connect appointments to encounters, introduce the patient queue system, and eventually build personalized clinic websites on top of the existing infrastructure.
This approach makes Medframe a genuine clinical management platform, rather than a booking platform that adds medical features afterward.
