# Station patient kiosk and department visits

Authorized by the existing Station brief and the user's explicit native implementation request. Continue Phase 2; no separate chat or subagents. Inherit PRODUCT.md/DESIGN.md and the established Station visual system. Preserve original data/credentials/MFA, Django permissions, existing clinical aggregate and rollback. Phase 3 provider-confirmed digital payments/TV remains separate.

A paired, approved kiosk opens four touch actions: booking, check-in, walk-in, payments. Existing patient identity is established by a one-use five-minute code issued by their authenticated verified portal link, or authorized reception after recording in-person identity verification. Pairing/name/date-of-birth do not grant patient identity. A digest-only HttpOnly device-bound patient session expires after two minutes idle and ten minutes absolute; explicit activity extension is bounded by the absolute deadline. Live issuer/link/device revocation is checked. New registration submits only required name/contact/optional birth date to an intake review request; authorized staff select an existing record or explicitly create one after duplicate review. No automatic linking by demographic match and no kiosk patient password/MFA changes.

Reuse provider schedules, reservations, appointments and consultation queue tickets. Kiosk booking lists one configured consultation service/provider/date with real available slots, reviews before idempotent confirmation, and does not admit to today's queue. Check-in accepts only the verified patient's same-branch appointment within configured early/late arrival window and creates one visit/stage/ticket. Walk-in chooses a configured department; consultation needs a real eligible provider, other stages do not. Counters issue unique department/day labels. One open patient/branch/day visit owns stage history, can link one existing consultation ticket and never creates a competing encounter. Repeated admission and confirmation cannot duplicate tickets/appointments. Existing consultation service actions remain authoritative and are synchronized into the stage feed.

Authorized staff configure departments, issue identity codes, review intake, call/recall/skip/prioritize/cancel/complete/transfer stages with optimistic versions and attributed events. Branch/provider permissions remain enforced. Transfers retain the visit identity and stage history and reconcile a linked consultation ticket through its existing state transition owner. No fabricated doctor for laboratory/cashier. Ticket shows number, department, issue time and next step; reliable waiting estimates are unavailable and are not invented. Browser printing is explicit optional device printing, with issuance independent of print success.

After proof, payment view reads only issued same-patient/branch invoice lines and totals. Digital/cash hardware acceptance are unavailable until configured and verified in Phase 3. The working alternative is an idempotent cashier stage in the same visit; a kiosk button never records payment or issues a paid receipt.

UI: generous white/navy/teal task home, four large labelled actions, readable step heading and summary, native shadcn controls, Back/Home/Cancel/staff assistance, warning before inactivity expiry, accessible extend/finish actions. Patient state remains in memory, never URL/local storage; end/timeout clears server session/cookie, model state and pending requests. No previous patient restored by browser Back/bfcache. Poll device status without extending patient activity; stale/revoked/offline state clears patient content and requires staff assistance. Staff management lives beside existing Devices & Stations and links to existing consultation queue management.

Verification is one accumulated focused backend/build/type/lint/browser pass, at most one necessary activation confirmation. Log unrelated findings and honest coverage gaps. Activate locally with fresh encrypted checkpoint/verified restore, additive migrations and exact original row preservation. Never import review records into original or reset original data.
