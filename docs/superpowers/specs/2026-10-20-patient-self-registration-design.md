# Patient-first registration — email-first

User direction: a patient verifies contact, completes registration and waits for provider acceptance. The user selected email first; mobile verification remains unavailable until SMS is configured. Execute natively, preserve existing records/MFA/access and use visual-only bounded verification, with no production build or automated tests.

Each authorized clinic branch can create, copy and disable one reusable public registration link. Patients open `/portal/register/[token]`, see the clinic/branch, enter their email and confirm a six-digit code before the demographic form is available. Codes expire after ten minutes, are session/link-bound, attempt-limited and stored as keyed hashes; outbox delivery uses existing local email. Resend invalidates prior codes. Email verification proves control of contact only and never grants patient/clinical access. A verified proof permits one idempotent registration submission. Minimal demographics and self/guardian relationship enter a pending queue; no account, patient record or portal grant is created at submission.

Patients see a pending/accepted/declined status in their session. Staff with live `portal.manage` plus demographic read may review requests in their permitted branch. Approval requires identity/relationship attestation and either explicit creation (existing register permission) or selection of an existing authorized matching patient. Possible duplicates block new record creation, including inaccessible matches without exposing their details. Acceptance delegates to native registration and native portal invitation within one transaction, then sends the account-access continuation automatically. Existing invitation/account/password/MFA owners remain authoritative. Decline creates no patient/link. Decisions are versioned, attributed and audited; retries cannot create duplicate registrations/invitations. Guardian requests must use the native verified-guardian invitation method. No automatic matching by shared email/phone, credentials reset, new roles or grant bypass.

UI: public three-step Verify email → Patient details → Clinic review, readable cards, six individual OTP cells, clear expiry/resend/error states and truthful SMS unavailability. Staff open Registration requests from Patients, choose a permitted branch, manage the reusable link and review pending requests with a focused approval form. The legacy individual invitation remains for staff-assisted exceptions.

Implementation changes are additive portal tables/endpoints plus focused frontend modules. The launcher must checkpoint before additive migration and restart original Django services, keeping the dev frontend and all unrelated working-tree changes. Email delivery remains local; no external SMS or email-provider configuration is authorized.
