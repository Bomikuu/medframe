# Clinic Services

Mode: Operate. Extend the incumbent Medframe white/navy/teal workspace.
First viewport: services list with price/payment/workflow summaries; branch selection and clear Add service. Patient services has its own tab. No promotional hero or decorative image.
Editor: grouped service details, ordered workflow, payment preset. Workflow reader never sees prices without a financial permission. Patient case presents a readable checklist and attributed history; agreement review shows the entire dated schedule before issue.
Responsive: compact list stacks on phone, sheets are full-width, schedule tables scroll within their own region; tablet retains useful side-by-side fields where space permits.

Patient service refinement: payment context reads the existing scoped invoice, shows net received, outstanding, due and next unpaid payment, with a Manage payments destination. Native draft/void states are explicit. Schedule filters are presentation-only, preserve original monthly numbering and leave one due summary. Shared staff/portal renderer uses the invoice state for voided labels.
