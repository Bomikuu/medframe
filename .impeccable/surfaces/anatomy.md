Inherit Medframe's navy, white/cool canvas, teal selection, Manrope/Jakarta typography and real shadcn controls. Keep the same consultation FormProvider, immutable source/side/anchor metadata and existing save/finalization. Enter the anatomy tab as a focused workspace. No medical advice, fabricated conversation, network AI request, clinical-note copying or persistent chat store. Measurement/cross-section/organs/systems remain visibly unavailable. Group labels are navigation aids, not clinical metadata. Use exact real model renders for view previews and retain attribution.
