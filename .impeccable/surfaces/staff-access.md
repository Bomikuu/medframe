# Staff & Access

Mode: Operate. Preserve the Medframe cool canvas, white surfaces, navy actions, teal states and native typography. Settings → Staff & Access is the administrator’s team destination. Search/filterable cards use circular private portraits/initials, clear role/title/branch/workspace/billing summaries and one Manage entry. Separate Manage and Invite dialogs group identity/photo, role/branch, workspace/billing and doctor specialty. Role and active/mode controls use actual Switch semantics. No fabricated faces.

Staff onboarding follows invitation → email OTP → profile/photo → account/native MFA → workspace, using a quiet step rail, purposeful icons and a single task card. Existing identity/MFA are retained. Photos auto-select after sanitation/scan; no gallery or manual Use photo step. Keep recipient identity, assigned branch/roles and Station restriction clear. Use explicit inline errors and native recovery; local email helper is development-only.

“All permitted tools” never means unrestricted patient records. Approved-Station-only is server enforced. Billing levels and patient grants stay separate. No global role editor, secret credential entry by administrators, or platform access grants. See actual bounded verification in docs/operations/staff-access-verification.md; authenticated/security/photo/MFA acceptance is not established by synthetic visuals.
