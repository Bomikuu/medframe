# Patient self-registration

Operate mode. Native email-first registration at `/portal/register/[token]`, from one enabled, reusable branch link managed through Patients → Registration requests. Preserve patient records, permissions and native portal-account/MFA workflows. Separate contact verification from clinic identity review and final portal access. Shared cool canvas/circle-line pattern, white cards, restrained icons and teal current/complete stages; readable errors, expiry/resend states and six individual OTP cells. Phone form uses one column. After submitting, show pending review and a manual Check status action; no reload loop. SMSGate mobile verification and Google sign-up show explicit unavailable states until configured. Mobile proof requires a separate, clearly unverified invitation email; Google contact proof still leads to clinic review. Gateway acceptance must never imply handset delivery. Approval is an explicit staff-scoped action with duplicate review and automatic native portal invitation. Guardian authority is reviewed, never inferred from contact ownership.

Phones use a separate +63 calling-code control and digit-filtered national input with automatic spacing; accept domestic/international pasted numbers and emit compact international values to native forms. Email typing/paste is normalized through shared Input. Maintain the same phone control in clinic/patient/guardian/Station/communication/website contact forms, with labels tied to the national number and a distinct Country calling code label.
